## Reading Resources

### Books
- [ ] **Chip Huyen — AI Engineering** (O'Reilly, 2025) — RAG, agents, evals, deployment end to end

- [ ] **Sebastian Raschka — Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch)** — how LLMs work internally
