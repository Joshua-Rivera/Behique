# Watching the night sky

## Meteor showers
The Perseids peak around 12 August and can produce 60 meteors an hour under dark skies. The Geminids in mid December are often even richer.

## Telescopes
A 200 mm Dobsonian is the classic first telescope: large aperture for the price and simple to set up. Let it cool outside for half an hour before observing.
