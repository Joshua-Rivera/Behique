# Houseplant care

## Monstera
Water when the top five centimetres of soil are dry. Yellow leaves usually mean overwatering. Give it a moss pole so the aerial roots can climb.

## Snake plant
Tolerates low light and neglect. Water roughly once a month in winter. Root rot is the main way people kill it.

## Fiddle leaf fig
Hates being moved. Drops leaves when the light or temperature changes suddenly.
