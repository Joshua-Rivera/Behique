# Portugal trip, May

## Getting around
The Alfa Pendular train from Lisbon Santa Apolónia to Porto Campanhã takes just under three hours. Book in advance on the CP website for the cheapest fares.

## Food
In Porto, try a francesinha, a sandwich covered in melted cheese and a beer-tomato sauce. In Lisbon, the pastéis de nata at Manteigaria are worth the queue.

## Budget
Accommodation averaged 85 euros a night. Total spend for nine days was about 1,400 euros.
