# Sourdough baking log

## Starter maintenance
Feed the starter twice a day with equal weights of flour and water. A healthy starter doubles in four to six hours at room temperature. If it smells like nail polish remover, it is hungry.

## Bulk fermentation
After mixing, do four sets of stretch and folds thirty minutes apart. Bulk is done when the dough has grown by about half and shows bubbles on the sides of the container.

## Baking
Bake in a preheated Dutch oven at 250 °C for 20 minutes with the lid on, then 25 minutes uncovered until the crust is deep brown.
